## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the high-stakes precision of orbital command centers, deep-space telemetry arrays, and advanced aerospace heads-up displays (HUDs). Designed for engineers, payload specialists, and mission operators, it prioritizes clarity in low-light environments, instantaneous signal recognition, and tactical calm under critical anomalies.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Technical Glassmorphism** with **Aerospace Instrument Minimalism**. Translucent frosted overlays simulate pressurized optical instrumentation without obstructing multi-layered telemetry streams. Crisp geometric titling pairs with high-legibility system typography to organize vast metric densities into readable flight tiers. The emotional posture is strictly professional, highly capable, and technologically uncompromising.

## Colors

The palette operates within a controlled deep-space radiance model:
- **Base Canvas (`neutral_color_hex`: `#030712`)**: Deep obsidian base minimizing screen glare during prolonged observation shifts.
- **Primary Radar & Vectors (`primary_color_hex`: `#06b6d4`)**: Electric cyan denoting operational paths, nominal telemetry states, active targeting locks, and verified telemetry uplinks.
- **Secondary Caution & Warning (`secondary_color_hex`: `#f59e0b`)**: Tactical orbital amber deployed strictly for threshold alerts, sensor warnings, and delta-v throttles.
- **Tertiary Accent (`tertiary_color_hex`: `#22d3ee`)**: Fluorescent cyan reserved for active radar sweeps, real-time focus rings, and high-frequency telemetry pulses.

Layering follows strict luminescence hierarchy: deep matte backdrops support low-opacity glass panels (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`), framed by subtle photon borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`), while cyan and amber glows convey vital telemetry changes.

## Typography

Typography establishes systematic hierarchy between strategic overviews and granular sensor logs:
- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`)**: Geometric, technical, and forward-looking. Used for mission states, module titles, and critical waypoint classifications. All labels and callouts adopt uppercase tracking for quick scanning.
- **Operational Copy & Readouts (`Inter`)**: Engineered for effortless legibility under dense informational payloads, tabular streams, and orbital coordinate feeds.
- **Numbers and Vectors**: Numeric tables and telemetry streams should apply tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent horizontal jitter during rapid real-time updates.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on an instrumental 12-column fluid grid tailored for wide-format telemetry consoles and tactical mobile terminals:
- **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: 12 columns with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. High horizontal real estate houses concurrent telemetry streams, split-spectrum radars, and orbital path charts side-by-side.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8 columns with `margin: 1.5rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Sub-monitors collapse into unified scrollable stacks.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with `margin: 1rem` and `gutter: 0.75rem`. Non-critical radar monitors collapse into tabbed overlays to preserve vital mission trajectory logs.

Component spacing follows a modular 4px base increment (`space-xs` through `space-xl`), ensuring compact layouts without sacrificing instrument delineation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses optical glassmorphism combined with tactical glow vectors rather than traditional drop shadows:

- **Surface Level 0 (Deep Canvas)**: Solid `#030712` base.
- **Surface Level 1 (Tactical Console Modules)**: Translucent glass layers (`background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`) with hardware-accelerated backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`), reinforced by structural ghost borders (`border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).
- **Surface Level 2 (Floating Pods & Popovers)**: Enhanced optical density (`background: rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`), framed by ambient cyan radiation (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px -5px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25)`).
- **Surface Level 3 (Emergency Overlays & Intercept Modals)**: Border-accented glass with high-intensity vector highlights (`box-shadow: 0 0 35px -5px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.35)` when cautionary).

## Shapes

The design uses soft, precision-machined geometry (`roundedness: 1`). Core UI cards and instrument enclosures feature minimal 0.25rem (`4px`) radii, providing subtle structural softening while maintaining the clean silhouette of aviation monitors. Floating action panels utilize `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`), while status pills and analog telemetry tags scale to `0.75rem` (`rounded-xl`).

## Components

### Buttons & Tactical Triggers
- **Primary Telemetry Trigger**: Filled with radiant cyan (`#06b6d4`), text set in dark obsidian (`#030712`) using `Space Grotesk` Bold with subtle cyan backglow (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.4)`).
- **Ghost/Auxiliary Action**: Translucent glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`) with crisp borders (`1px solid rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.4)`). Hover states elevate border brightness to `#22d3ee` and text to `#ffffff`.
- **Warning/Abort Action**: Deep crimson-tinted glass accented with vibrant amber borders (`#f59e0b`), accompanied by an outer warning glow.

### Chips & Telemetry Badges
- Precision micro-containers with uppercase labels (`label-sm`).
- **Nominal Signal**: Translucent cyan background (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.12)`) with a solid cyan stroke (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.4)`) and glowing status dot.
- **Planetary Analog Indicator**: Minimal pill format displaying target gravitational and atmospheric metrics with muted mono data points.

### Radar & Circular Scanners
- Concentric HUD rings rendered with fine lines (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.2)`).
- Dynamic radial sweep using conical cyan gradients (`rgba(34, 211, 238, 0.25)` to transparent). Target blips marked by expanding ping animations.

### Input Fields & Command Prompts
- Low-profile structural fields (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`) with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` borders.
- On focus: Borders shift to `#06b6d4` with an inner glow (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 8px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.2)`). Labels rest above inputs in uppercase `label-md`.

### Cards & Module Panels
- Wrapped in backdrop-blur glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`) with corner indexing cues (tactical crosshairs or micro-bracket accents along panel corners).
- Segmented headers bordered by a fine separator line (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), pairing a module status dot with a `title-md` header.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches
- Checkboxes maintain a square silhouette with chamfered edges; selected states illuminate in `#06b6d4`.
- Track toggles utilize deep-space obsidian troughs framed by fine borders; active toggles slide a glowing cyan nodule into position.
